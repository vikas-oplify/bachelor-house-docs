# Buying a plan

Priya's Free listing ended and she wants **Prime Connect** for her flat. The same steps apply to every plan; only what it's bought for changes (a listing, a city, or the account).

## 1. See the plans

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> `/for-owner` (shows ₹799 for Prime Connect; the catalogue says ₹2,499)

She sees owner and PG plans only, because she's an owner. [Plans & pricing](../product/money/plans-and-pricing.mdx)

## 2. Billing details

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

Optional. She saves her name, address and state, and a GSTIN if she has one. The GSTIN is checked for format, checksum and state code.

## 3. Apply a coupon

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

She enters `DIWALI20`. The website shows the discount, taxable value, GST and total. Nothing is reserved yet. [Coupons](../product/money/coupons.mdx)

## 4. Create the order

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

The server prices it from the plan version: ₹2,499 − 20% = ₹1,999.20, plus 9% CGST and 9% SGST because she's in Maharashtra. The coupon use is reserved. A Razorpay order is created and she gets an order number like `BHO-261030-00042`. She has **30 minutes** to pay. Pressing Buy again returns the same order.

## 5. Pay

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

She pays in the Razorpay checkout. Card details never reach BachelorHouse.

## 6. Verify and fulfil

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

The website sends Razorpay's callback. The server checks the signature, fetches the payment amount from Razorpay itself, and fulfils the order **once**: the subscription starts and the coupon use is consumed. If the payment is authorised but not captured yet, she sees "Pending verification".

If she closes the browser right after paying, nothing picks the payment up: there's no Razorpay webhook or reconcile job yet.

## 7. Invoice

**Backend** <Status s="none" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

The order is queued for a GST invoice through Zoho, but nothing processes the queue yet.

## 8. The plan takes effect

**Backend** <Status s="none" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

Not connected. The subscription exists, but listing submit and reactivate don't read it, so her flat still gets `PLAN_REQUIRED`. The same goes for agent slots and tenant contacts. [Known limitations](../product/known-limitations.md)

## If something goes wrong

| What happens | Result |
| --- | --- |
| She doesn't pay in 30 minutes | The order expires and the coupon use is released |
| She pays after it expired | The payment is flagged for finance; no plan starts |
| Staff reject a listing she paid for before it ever went live | A full refund is queued (paid manually today) |
| She tries to delete her account mid-payment | Refused until the order settles |
