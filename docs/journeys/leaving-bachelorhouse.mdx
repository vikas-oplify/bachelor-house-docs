Arjun found a flat and wants his data gone. This journey works end to end in the backend; the website has no screens for it yet.

## 1. Ask what BachelorHouse holds

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

He can raise an **access** request first (or correction, consent withdrawal, grievance). It's due in 30 days and he can see its status. [Privacy and deletion](../product/accounts/privacy-and-deletion.mdx)

## 2. Delete the account

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

He asks to delete his account. If he has a payment in progress or a paid order not yet fulfilled, it's refused until that settles.

## 3. Straight away

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

His account is closed, any listings are taken off the site, every device is signed out, and his phone number is freed.

## 4. After 30 days

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

A daily job erases his name, email, billing details, uploads, KYC files and newsletter subscriptions. Staff can do it sooner by closing his erasure request.

## 5. What stays

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

- His orders, payments and invoices, for 8 years by law.
- A one-way hash of his number in the free-allowance record. If he signs up again, he starts with **no** free credits.
- The audit log, which was masked when written.
