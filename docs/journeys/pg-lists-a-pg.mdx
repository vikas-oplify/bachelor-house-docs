# PG lister lists a PG

Ravi runs a 20-room boys' PG in Hadapsar with double and triple rooms. He signs up as an owner. This is every step he goes through today.

## 1. Sign in as an owner

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> `/signup`

Same as any owner: phone OTP, then the profile as **owner**. An agent account can also list a PG, but [needs a PG plan and approval](./agent-lists-for-owners.mdx).

## 2. Describe the PG and its rooms

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> `/pg/post-property` with the room pop-up

- **Basic:** PG name, contact name, gender (male), tenant type, notice and lock-in days, 20 rooms: 12 double and 8 triple.
- **Location:** city, a locality picked from the list (he can't type a new one), address.
- **Rooms:** one entry per configuration, e.g. "Double with AC" ₹6,500 per bed, 6 rooms, 2 free. The double configurations add up to 12.
- **Amenities:** house rules, services, security, meals.

[Field rules](../product/listing/posting-a-pg.mdx)

## 3. Photos

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" />

He sorts photos into categories. He needs at least one **building** photo, one **double room** and one **triple room** photo, at most 3 per category and 20 in all. A private-room photo would be refused because he doesn't offer private rooms.

## 4. KYC

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> KYC step of `/pg/post-property`

He uploads his **ID proof** (only the last 4 characters of the number are kept), **ownership proof** and a **selfie at the PG**. Files go to a private store. If he lists a second PG at the same address later, this KYC covers it. [PG KYC](../product/listing/pg-kyc.mdx)

## 5. Submit

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

The same checks as a flat. His [Free listing](../product/listing/free-listing.mdx) is reserved. **Every PG goes to review.** If his KYC isn't approved yet, the listing carries a "KYC pending" blocker.

## 6. Review

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" /> (admin API only)

A moderator reviews the KYC (through 60-second file links) and the listing. Approving the KYC lifts the blocker; approving the listing puts it live. Rejecting the KYC of a PG that's already live hides it until a new KYC is approved.

## 7. Live

**Backend** <Status s="partial" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> `/pg`, `/pg/properties` with sample data

Live for **30 days from approval** on the Free listing. As with flats, there's no search or detail API yet, so tenants can't find it. After 30 days he needs a PG plan (PG Start ₹1,499 and up), which [doesn't attach to listings yet](../product/known-limitations.md).

## 8. Keep rooms up to date

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

He changes "rooms free" per configuration whenever a bed fills or opens. **No new review**, and his plan clock isn't touched. When the last room fills, the PG is **full**: tenants can set a [room alert](../product/tenants/pg-room-alerts.mdx). When he opens a room again, each waiting tenant gets one in-app message.

## 9. Weekly check-in

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

Every **7 days** without a confirmation he gets an in-app reminder at 10:00 IST. One tap confirms. Without it, the PG shows "Availability not recently verified" and its quality score's availability points drop after 15 days.
