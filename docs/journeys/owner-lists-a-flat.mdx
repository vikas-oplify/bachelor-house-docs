## 1. Sign in with her phone

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> `/signup`

She enters her mobile number, gets a 6-digit code by SMS (valid 5 minutes) and enters it. A new account is created in "profile pending" state. [Sign-in details](../product/accounts/sign-in-and-profile.mdx)

## 2. Complete her profile as an owner

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> part of `/signup`

Name, optional email, **I am an owner**, the 18+ box, terms and privacy. The account type is fixed from now on. If she gave an email, a 24-hour verification link goes out.

## 3. Start a draft and fill the 5 steps

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> `/post-property`

She starts a flat listing and fills **Basic**, **Location**, **Rental**, **Amenities** in any order. Each step saves on its own. Her flat number and exact pin stay private. Writing her phone number in the description is refused. [Field rules](../product/listing/posting-a-flat.mdx)

## 4. Upload photos

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> step 5 of `/post-property`

Each photo goes straight to storage, is resized and watermarked in the background, then attached with a tag. She needs 5 to 20 photos, every one tagged, and one cover. She may add one video. [Photos and video](../product/listing/photos-and-video.mdx)

## 5. Submit

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

The checks run in order: every step valid, no contact details, [quality score](../product/listing/quality-score.mdx) at least 40, not a duplicate property. She has no plan, so her **[Free listing](../product/listing/free-listing.mdx) is reserved** for this flat. The listing goes to **review** (every owner listing does).

If her score is 35, she gets the top 3 tips instead, for example "Add more amenities and furnishing items".

While it's in review she can **withdraw** it to a draft; the Free listing is released.

## 6. Staff review

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" /> (admin API only)

A moderator claims the item, looks at the listing and its flags, and decides. [Staff side](./staff-review-a-listing.mdx)

| Decision | What Priya sees |
| --- | --- |
| Approve | In-app: approved. The listing is live. |
| Request changes | In-app, with the moderator's note. She withdraws to a draft, fixes it, submits again. |
| Reject | In-app, with the note. Her Free listing is free to use again. |

## 7. Live for 30 days

**Backend** <Status s="partial" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> listing pages show sample data

The listing is `active` for **30 days from approval** and the Free listing is now used. But there's **no search or listing page API yet**, so no tenant can find it.

While live she can **pause** it (the 30 days keep running), **mark it rented**, **archive** it, or **confirm it's still available** (which keeps her quality score up). [Listing lifecycle](../product/listing/listing-lifecycle.mdx)

## 8. Enquiries

**Backend** <Status s="none" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" /> (owner dashboard not built)

When built: tenants who spend a credit appear as locked enquiries; she picks up to **2** to reveal. A plan unlocks all of them. [Contacting a lister](../product/tenants/contacting-a-lister.mdx)

## 9. Expiry and renewal

**Backend** <Status s="partial" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> `/for-owner` plan cards

- **3 days before** the end she gets an in-app reminder.
- At the end the listing becomes `expired` within a minute.
- To keep it live she needs an owner plan (Starter Boost ₹1,499 and up). She can [buy one](./buying-a-plan.mdx), but **a bought plan doesn't attach to the listing yet**, so reactivating returns `PLAN_REQUIRED`.

## What blocks this journey today

1. No listing search or detail, so step 7 has no audience.
2. No enquiries (step 8).
3. Paid plans don't reach listings (step 9).
4. The website isn't connected at any step.
