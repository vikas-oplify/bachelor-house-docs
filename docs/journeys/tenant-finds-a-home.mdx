Arjun is moving to Pune for a job in Hinjewadi and needs a flat share or PG. This is his journey, and how much of it works today.

## 1. Sign in as a tenant

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> `/signup`

Phone OTP, then the profile as **tenant**. His number gets **5 free contact credits for life**. If he later deletes the account and signs up again, he doesn't get 5 more.

## 2. Pick an area

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> hero search on `/` (pickers do nothing yet)

He types "hinj" and autocomplete suggests Hinjewadi and the IT parks there. Or he taps **Use my location** and gets his nearest locality. [Search and places](../product/tenants/search-and-places.mdx)

## 3. Search

**Backend** <Status s="none" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> `/properties`, `/pg/properties` with sample data

Not built. The listing index, quality score and ranking configuration exist, but there's no search endpoint.

## 4. Open a listing

**Backend** <Status s="none" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> `/properties/[id]`, `/pg/properties/[id]`

Not built. When it is, the page shows the public pin (moved 50 to 300 m) and never the lister's phone number.

## 5. Contact the lister

**Backend** <Status s="none" /> · **Website** <Status s="screen" /> Contact Owner and View Number buttons (do nothing)

Not built. The decided rule: **one credit per listing**, the first time only. Calling, then WhatsApping the same listing costs one credit. The response carries the lister's name, phone and WhatsApp link. [Contacting a lister](../product/tenants/contacting-a-lister.mdx)

## 6. A PG is full

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

On a full PG he taps **Notify me**. It costs nothing and lasts 60 days. When the PG lister opens a room, he gets one in-app message. [PG room alerts](../product/tenants/pg-room-alerts.mdx)

## 7. Run out of credits

**Backend** <Status s="partial" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" /> (no tenant plans page)

He can buy **BH Connect** (15 contacts, ₹999), **BH Plus** (30, ₹1,499) or **BH Max** (50, ₹2,199). [Checkout](./buying-a-plan.mdx) works, but there's nothing to spend the contacts on until enquiries exist.

## 8. Chat and visit

**Backend** <Status s="none" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

Not built. Chat will live inside the enquiry, with limits of 30 messages per conversation per hour and 200 a day, and an email if a message stays unread for 15 minutes.

## What blocks this journey today

Steps 3, 4, 5 and 8 are the core of the marketplace and none of them exist. This is the biggest gap in the product.
