Neha is a BachelorHouse moderator. Ravi's PG from the [PG journey](./pg-lists-a-pg.mdx) is waiting. There's no admin website yet, so every step below is an admin API call.

## 1. Sign in with Google

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

She signs in with her Google account. Her session ends **8 hours** after sign-in. A super admin gave her the **moderator** role. [Staff roles](../product/platform/staff-roles.mdx)

## 2. Open the queue

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

She sees listings, media and KYC items, oldest and highest priority first. Ravi's PG is there with the reason `pg`. A support colleague wouldn't see listing items at all.

## 3. Claim the item

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

She claims it for **30 minutes**. Another moderator trying to claim it is refused. If she walks away, it goes back to the queue automatically.

## 4. Check the KYC

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

She opens Ravi's KYC submission, which lists the documents with no links. She asks for a **60-second link** to each file, with a reason; each request is audited. She approves the KYC. [PG KYC](../product/listing/pg-kyc.mdx)

## 5. Decide

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

She **approves** the listing with a reason code and a note. The PG goes live, its 30 Free days start, and the Free allowance is used. She could instead request changes, reject or suspend. [Moderation](../product/platform/moderation.mdx)

## 6. The lister is told

**Backend** <Status s="built" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

Ravi gets an in-app message, and the listing history shows the change with her note.

## 7. Refund if needed

**Backend** <Status s="partial" /> · **Website** <Status s="none" />

Had she rejected a listing Ravi had paid a plan for (before it ever went live), a full refund would be queued. Nothing processes refunds yet, so finance would pay it manually.
