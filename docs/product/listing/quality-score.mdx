# Quality score

Every listing has a **quality score from 0 to 100**. It decides whether the listing can be submitted at all, and whether paid placement applies. The lister sees the score with up to three tips on how to raise it.

## What earns points

| Component | Max points | Full points when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Completeness | 25 | Every optional field is filled and the description is 150+ characters |
| Photos | 25 | Many sharp photos (long edge 1,200 px+) covering the key rooms. Duplicates count once. 3 photos earn 6 of the count points, 5 earn 10, 8+ earn 15. |
| Pricing | 20 | Rent, deposit and maintenance are filled, and the rent sits inside the usual range for similar listings |
| Amenities | 15 | 8 or more amenities and furnishing items |
| Location | 10 | The map pin is inside the chosen locality, with a society or landmark |
| Availability | 5 | Confirmed available in the last 15 days (60% of the points up to 30 days, none after) |

The maxima are part of a versioned ranking configuration that a super admin can change. They always add up to 100.

## What the score unlocks

| Score | Band | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Below 40 | | Can't be submitted (`QUALITY_TOO_LOW`) |
| 40 to 59 | Organic | Visible, but paid placement is switched off |
| 60 to 79 | Paid | Paid benefits (featured badge, promotion) apply |
| 80 and up | Full | Full benefits apply |

If a live listing with a paid plan drops below 60, its featured or premium tier is cleared. The plan keeps running and the tier can come back once the score is 60 again.

## Tips

The lister sees the three components with the most points still available, biggest first. For example: "Add more amenities and furnishing items (8 or more earns the full points)".

## When it's recomputed

- When the listing changes, and as part of submit, so the dashboard and the submit error always agree.
- Every night at 03:00 IST for every live listing, because comparable rents and availability age.

## Edge cases worth testing

- A listing at exactly 40: can be submitted.
- Two identical photos counted as one: score lower than with two different photos.
- A pin dropped outside the locality: location points drop.
- A live listing not confirmed for 31 days: availability points go to 0 overnight.
