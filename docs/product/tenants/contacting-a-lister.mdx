# Contacting a lister

A lister's phone number is what tenants pay for. A tenant sees it only after spending **one contact credit** on that listing, and it never appears on a listing or profile page.

:::info[Not built yet]
The rules below are decided (ADR 0008 in the backend repo) and the credit ledger exists, but there is **no enquiry endpoint yet**. The website's Call, WhatsApp and Contact buttons do nothing. Treat this page as the spec the build will follow.
:::

## How it will work

1. The tenant taps **Call**, **WhatsApp** or **Chat** on an active listing.
2. The backend checks the listing is active and hasn't passed its end date.
3. If the tenant already contacted this listing, nothing is charged: the existing enquiry just records another event.
4. Otherwise **one credit** is used: a free credit first, then a package credit.
5. The response carries the lister's name, phone and a WhatsApp link. They're also kept in the tenant's **My contacts**.
6. The lister is notified.

Chat is part of the enquiry, so the same gate applies to it.

## Credits

| Source | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | **5 for life** | Tied to the phone number, like the [Free listing](../listing/free-listing.mdx). Survives account deletion. |
| BH Connect | 15 contacts · ₹999 · 45 days | |
| BH Plus | 30 contacts · ₹1,499 · 60 days | Priority enquiries |
| BH Max | 50 contacts · ₹2,199 · 90 days | Priority enquiries |

A tenant has **one package at a time**. Buying a new one replaces the current one, whatever its tier, and unused contacts from the old one lapse. Staff can [give credits back](../platform/configurable-rules.mdx#staff-overrides), up to 100 per grant.

## Free listings: the owner chooses

On an owner's Free listing, enquiries start **locked**. The owner picks up to **2** to reveal and sees those tenants' contact details. A paid plan unlocks all enquiries.

## When the lister sees the tenant

The lister's view of a tenant's contact details is worked out fresh on every request, never stored as "unlocked". It needs one of: an active plan on the listing, an agent's city plan, the enquiry's 90-day window, or a Free reveal. The tenant must also have given contact-sharing consent and still be active.

## Limits

| Limit | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Enquiries per tenant per hour | 10 |
| Enquiries per tenant per day | 30 |
| Chat messages per conversation per hour | 30 |
| Chat messages per day | 200 |
| Email about an unread chat message | After 15 minutes |
| Chat kept for | 12 months |

All are [settings](../platform/configurable-rules.mdx).

## Edge cases to test once built

- Contact the same listing by call, then WhatsApp: one credit.
- Contact with 0 credits left: `QUOTA_EXHAUSTED`.
- Two taps at once on a new listing: one credit.
- Contact a listing one second after its end.
- Listing, profile and search responses never contain a phone field.
