# Search and places

The place data behind search is built: cities, localities, landmarks and societies, with typo-tolerant autocomplete. **Searching listings is not built.** The website's listing pages (`/properties`, `/pg/properties`) and detail pages show sample data.

## Places

| Place | What it is | Who manages it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| City | Pune is seeded. A second city can be added by staff. | Staff |
| Locality | Areas within a city, e.g. Baner, Hadapsar | Staff. Listers of flats can request a new one. |
| Landmark | Colleges, IT parks, metro and railway stations, bus stands, malls, hospitals, office hubs | Staff |
| Society | Housing societies, with aliases and a map pin | Seeded for Pune. Staff rename, fix aliases and merge duplicates. A lister can type a society that isn't listed; it's saved as text on the listing. |

Inactive places are hidden from customers but never deleted.

## Autocomplete

- Suggests localities, landmarks and societies within a city.
- Starts at **3 characters**, returns up to **10** suggestions.
- Tolerates typos ("banr" finds Baner).

## Use my location

The website sends the device's coordinates once and gets back the nearest city and locality. A point more than **30 km** from every active locality is outside the areas BachelorHouse serves. The coordinates are not stored or logged.

## Listing search

Not built. What exists for it today:

- Every listing keeps a search text index, refreshed on submit and on change.
- The [quality score](../listing/quality-score.mdx) and a versioned ranking configuration are in place.
- Decided but not built: search snapshots with signed page cursors, paid placement (with a setting for whether it applies when the user picks a sort), homepage rotation, and tenant-to-listing matching (switched off).

## Edge cases worth testing

- Autocomplete with 2 characters: refused.
- "Use my location" from Mumbai: no city found.
- A society merged into another: autocomplete returns only the survivor.
