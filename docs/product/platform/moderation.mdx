# Moderation

Everything that needs a human look lands in one **moderation queue**: listings, media, KYC submissions, possible duplicate properties, and ownership disputes. Staff only see the subject types their role may review.

## Working the queue

1. **Open the queue**, oldest and highest priority first. Filter by subject, status and reason, or show only items you've claimed.
2. **Claim** an item. A claim lasts **30 minutes**, so two moderators don't review the same thing. A job every 5 minutes puts expired claims back in the queue.
3. **Decide**, with a **reason code** and a **note the lister sees**. Every decision is audited.
4. Or **release** the claim to hand it back.

## Decisions on a listing

| Decision | From | Listing becomes | Lister is told |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Approve** | In review | Live. A Free listing's 30 days start now, and the Free allowance is used. | Approved |
| **Request changes** | In review | Changes requested. The lister withdraws to a draft, fixes, and resubmits. | Changes requested, with the note |
| **Reject** | In review | Rejected. A reserved Free listing is released. Any paid order is queued for a full refund. | Rejected, with the note |
| **Suspend** | In review, live, paused | Suspended | Suspended, with the note |

Staff notes are shown to the lister in the listing's history. Notes written by the lister or by the system stay private.

## Other subjects

| Subject | What a decision does |
| --- | --- |
| Media (a photo or video) | Approve makes it public. Reject or suspend hides it. |
| KYC submission | Approve or reject only. See [PG KYC](../listing/pg-kyc.mdx). |
| Possible duplicate property | Recorded and audited; no automatic effect yet |
| Ownership dispute | Decided from the disputes screen. See [Ownership disputes](../agents/ownership-disputes.mdx). |
| Listing edits, agent profiles, agent verification, reviews, chat messages | Recorded and audited only. These features don't exist yet. |

## Why an item is in the queue

`pg`, `first_listing`, `agent_first_five`, `possible_duplicate`, or `auto_flag` with the flags raised (banned words, duplicate photos, rent outlier). See [Submitting a listing](../listing/submission-and-review.mdx#which-listings-are-reviewed).

## Edge cases worth testing

- Two moderators claim the same item: the second is refused.
- Claim, wait 31 minutes, another moderator claims: allowed.
- Approve a listing that the lister withdrew a moment ago: refused.
- A support user opens the queue: no listing items.
- Reject a listing with a paid order: refund queued (check the order, since refunds don't process yet).
